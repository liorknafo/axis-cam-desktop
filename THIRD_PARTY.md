# Third-party components

AXIS application and helper source are MIT licensed. Rust dependency licenses remain those of their respective authors; versions are locked in Cargo.lock. `licenses/NOTICES.md` lists every Rust crate linked into the app with the full text of its licenses; it is generated by `scripts/generate-notices.sh` (cargo-about, configured by `about.toml` and `about.hbs`) and copied to the app's `Contents/Resources/ThirdParty` directory. Regenerate it whenever Cargo.lock changes.

The distributed macOS STEP helper links dynamically to unmodified Homebrew Open CASCADE libraries and dependencies. Library copies are relocated with install_name_tool and ad-hoc signed. Their licenses and Homebrew version/source receipts are included in `Contents/Resources/ThirdParty`.

The CAD helper's source is `native/step_preview.cpp`. Rebuild it and the app with a C++17 compiler, Rust and an Open CASCADE installation: set OCCT_PREFIX and run the packaging script. The dynamically linked libraries can be replaced with compatible modified versions in `Contents/Frameworks`; re-sign the local bundle after replacing binaries. No license term of this application restricts modification or reverse engineering needed to debug changes to these libraries. No distribution signing credentials are required for local ad-hoc signing.

Format implementation references: [3MF core specification](https://github.com/3MFConsortium/spec_core), [3MF Production extension](https://github.com/3MFConsortium/spec_production), and [Open CASCADE](https://dev.opencascade.org/).

Stock surface meshing uses unmodified Fidget, fidget-core and fidget-mesh 0.4.3 under MPL-2.0. Their corresponding source is available at [Fidget v0.4.3](https://github.com/mkeeter/fidget/tree/v0.4.3), or the crates.io archives for those exact locked versions. Rebuild with Cargo to replace these libraries. The license and source notice are distributed in `licenses/fidget-MPL-2.0.txt` and `licenses/fidget-NOTICE.txt`, and copied to the app's `Contents/Resources/Licenses` directory.

Polygon offsetting and clipping use the Clipper2 library by Angus Johnson, statically linked through the clipper2 and clipper2c-sys crates. Clipper2 and its C wrapper are under the Boost Software License 1.0 (`licenses/Clipper2-Boost.txt`, `licenses/clipper2c-Boost.txt`); the Rust crates are MIT or Apache-2.0 (`licenses/clipper2-MIT.txt`, `licenses/clipper2c-sys-MIT.txt`). All four texts are copied to the app's `Contents/Resources/Licenses` directory.

option-ext 0.2.0 (MPL-2.0, reached through dirs and dirs-sys, which find the user's data directories) is also used unmodified. Its source is available at [option-ext](https://github.com/soc/option-ext), or the crates.io archive for that exact locked version; rebuild with Cargo to replace it. Its MPL-2.0 text is in `licenses/fidget-MPL-2.0.txt` and in `licenses/NOTICES.md`. These four crates (Fidget's three and option-ext) are the only MPL-2.0 crates in the dependency tree.

HTTPS and token validation use rustls, reqwest and jsonwebtoken with the AWS-LC cryptographic library (aws-lc-rs, aws-lc-sys), statically linked. AWS-LC is distributed under the ISC, Apache-2.0 and OpenSSL licenses; its license text is in `licenses/aws-lc-LICENSE.txt` and is copied to the app's `Contents/Resources/Licenses` directory. Keychain access uses security-framework (MIT or Apache-2.0).
